# Brand voice — 12 Triangles / Dodici

The voice for the Dodici blog at 12triangles.com. 12 Triangles is a digital product
and AI services studio; Dodici is its editorial archive — "notes from building an AI
business partner in the real world."

## Who's reading
Founders, operators, and technical buyers evaluating whether 12 Triangles thinks
clearly about AI. The blog is editorial credibility, not the conversion path — it
earns trust that the contact page later cashes in. Write for a smart peer who is
skeptical of AI hype, not for a beginner who needs concepts explained.

## The stance
Practical over visionary. The studio builds systems that do real work; the writing
reflects that. We are not impressed by what AI *can* do — we care about what it
*reliably does* inside a real operating environment. Take positions. A Dodici post
should leave the reader with a claim they could disagree with.

## Sentence and structure habits (from the published work)
- **Short, declarative sentences.** State things plainly. Let a flat sentence land.
- **Parallel "X matters." constructions** for principle lists — `Continuity matters.`
  `Execution matters.` `Boundaries matters.` `Taste matters.` Each followed by one
  sentence of explanation.
- **"The goal is not X. The goal is Y."** Define by contrast — say what we're *not*
  doing before what we are. Used sparingly, it sharpens a thesis.
- **One pull-quote per post**, placed at the thesis peak, carrying the single idea
  you'd want quoted back to you.
- **Concrete operating nouns:** continuity, context, execution, momentum, judgment,
  follow-through, force multiplier, operating layer. Prefer these to abstract
  business-speak.
- **Forward-looking close.** End by pointing at what comes next, not by summarizing
  what was said.

## Vocabulary
- **Say:** practical, useful, continuity, execution, momentum, operating layer,
  force multiplier, real-world, reduce load, judgment, taste.
- **Avoid:** revolutionary, game-changer, unlock, supercharge, seamless, cutting-edge,
  "harness the power of," "in today's landscape," and anything that sounds like a
  product launch tweet.

## House habits to keep vs. cut
The existing `inside-dodici` post uses em-dashes heavily and a couple of
"not X, but Y" tricolons. Decision for new posts:
- **Keep:** the "goal is not / the goal is" contrast (it's load-bearing for the brand).
- **Use sparingly:** em-dashes — one or two per post, not as a default connector.
- **Cut:** stacked tricolons and reflexive "it's not just X, it's Y" — they read as AI
  filler. See `human-voice-checklist.md`.

## Gold-standard excerpts (match this texture)
> The goal is not to simulate a person. The goal is to build a practical operating
> layer that helps 12 Triangles move faster and with more clarity.

> In a small team, every context switch has a cost. Strategy, delivery, client work,
> internal tools, product bets, and experiments all compete for the same limited
> bandwidth.

> That is where Dodici starts to become interesting: not as a novelty, but as a force
> multiplier for focus, continuity, and follow-through.

## Format defaults for this blog
- `category`: `Case Study` for build write-ups; `Field Note` or `Operating Lesson`
  for shorter reflections.
- `tags`: always lead with `12 Triangles`, then 1–2 topic tags.
- Length: 350–700 words for a field note; longer for a case study. Density over length.
